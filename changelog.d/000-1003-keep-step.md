## 2026-11-14 (#000: 1003-keep-step — the nflverse keep step probed, and an unchanged answer takes the status of its payload (ARCHITECT, addendum 382 items 2-3).)
- **The keep step (382 item 2, #1003), probed fail-first on both paths.** The four faults: the payload directory cannot be made, the kept file's exclusive create fails, the copy into it fails for want of space, or the copy's source is gone.
  - **On a `--file`:** each fault now exits 2, with nothing kept and nothing written. The partial file and the folders the keep made and left empty are removed. The message names the error by its type and text, never by a path (`from_file`, with the new `_remove_empty_payload_dirs`). Before, each escaped `run()` as the raw `OSError`, its message carrying the full path, and the command exited 1, the failed-observation code.
  - **On a fetch:** the same faults were already a `failed` observation with nothing kept and no `.part` left, through fetch's own handler. The probe pins that.
- **The unchanged answer (382 item 3, verbatim: "AN UNCHANGED ANSWER TAKES THE STATUS OF THE PAYLOAD IT IS UNCHANGED FROM").** A 304, a byte-identical 200, a byte-identical `--file` or a restore now takes the status of the payload it is unchanged from: `empty` after a header-only payload, `complete` with rows_asserted 0 after a complete or partial one. It comes from `previous_observation` (the row `previous_payload` names, with its status) and `unchanged_status`. Nothing stored is rewritten; the rule holds from this merge.
- **docs/specs/availability-record.md:** the answers table's 304 and byte-identical lines say which status, and 382 item 3 is quoted beside the table; the `--file` paragraph carries the keep refusal.
