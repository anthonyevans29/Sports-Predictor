## 2026-11-10 (#000: 321-bulletin-hooks — THE BULLETIN HOOKS, STEP 1C (ARCHITECT 2026-11-10 13:05 ET, addendum 321 item 2; addendum 250 item 3): the closing run and the slate stage render their kept file's bulletin pairs into exports/bulletins/ before their own mirror push; a failure or refusal is receipted and notified, and nothing else changes)
- **Ruling (verbatim, addendum 321 item 2):** "THE HOOKS PR (step 1c) — OPEN IT, as #894's body item 1 shaped it: (i) in the closing run, between write_closing_file's success and push_mirror(); (ii) in the slate stage, between link_slate_file's success and push_mirror("slate"); each a subprocess of the command under a try; a failure or refusal receipted bulletin_failed / bulletin_refused and notified as a closing-file failure is; rec["exit"] never changed; the push proceeds with or without the pair; the laptop's MLB closing takes the same hook under laptop/bulletins/; test 6(e) with them; the console's URL lines unchanged. No timer and no chain step of its own: the hooks ride the closing and slate runs that exist."
- **Built:** deploy/hosting/closing.py `bulletin_hook` runs `python cli.py bulletin --closing-file|--slate-file <the kept file> --out-dir exports/bulletins` (`run_bulletin`, a subprocess in the checkout in its own session, bounded at `BULLETIN_TIMEOUT_S` = 90 s: the bound kills the render's whole process group, so nothing it started outlives the hook; a render that exited by the bound keeps its own exit even when something it left held the output pipe) right after the closing file's successful write and before `push_mirror()`, and right after `link_slate_file`'s success and before `push_mirror("slate")`. The run's receipt gains `bulletin` {command, exit, seconds, tail (the console's last four lines, redacted)[, error]}; exit 2 WITH the render's own `BULLETIN REFUSED` verdict line is `bulletin_refused`; any other exit, an exit 2 without that line (Python that cannot open cli.py, click's usage error: a broken checkout or command, never the guard), the bound or an exception is `bulletin_failed`; either is notified as the closing-file failure is (`bulletin_failure_notification`: "Closing <start>: PAGED; bulletin FAILED|REFUSED (<why>, <file>) · not run again"; the slate's "SLATE <FAMILY> <date>: LINKED; …" under its "<FAMILY> slate" title). `rec["exit"]` and `rec["failed"]` are never touched, nothing is run again, the push follows either way; the render's console (its path and mirror-URL lines) is printed as it printed it, every existing line unchanged. A closing whose file was not written, or whose page did not go out, renders nothing. The laptop's MLB closing takes the same hook; its push carries the pairs to laptop/bulletins/ by the machine's role (exports_mirror's kept subtree). tests/test_bulletin_hooks.py (21, addendum 250 item 6(e) among them, the real command on the synthetic store); the closing-run test frame fakes the render by default (`box.bulletin_on()` turns it on).
