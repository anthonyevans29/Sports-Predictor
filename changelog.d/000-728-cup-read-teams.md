## 2026-10-29 (#000: 728-cup-read-teams — intl-daily's cup schedule read refuses and says when a listing's club is not in the DB, naming the sync-teams to run; #728)
- **The ruling (ARCHITECT 2026-10-29 09:45 ET, verbatim):** "intl-daily's first FAC 2026/27 read skipped 21 listings (clubs not team-synced); the operator runs sync-teams FAC by hand today. The daily cup read should refuse-and-say, or be preceded by a sync-teams step for a competition-season with no teams, so the next new season does not repeat it: small PR after #709."
- cli.py `sync-matches`: `--refuse-unknown-teams` (new, opt-in). After the sync, a read that skipped a listing because a club is not in the DB prints one line and exits 1: `✗ REFUSED: <code> <season>: N listing(s) skipped, club(s) not in the DB (ids a..b); the known listings are written. Run: python cli.py sync-teams --competition <code> --season <season>`.
  - The listings with known clubs are written as without it; nothing is rolled back.
  - Only the service's `_skipped_ids` count (the ids its skip warning names), never another skip. A competition not in the DB is not this refusal.
  - Refused as a usage error with `--seasons` and on the MLB api-sports fallback.
- deploy/hosting/chains.py: intl-daily's four `CUP_SCHEDULE` steps (EFL, CL, UEL, FAC) pass the flag; no other step does. Every other `sync-matches` (the full-season chains, MLB's statsapi listings with the recurring M12 skips, the window, a by-hand run) is unchanged.
- **On the host:** a refusing cup step fails, so `sp_run.run_steps` stops intl-daily there (no `continue_on_sync_failure` outside the window chain). The cups after it in `CUP_SCHEDULE` go unread until sync-teams runs: open, needs-ruling (`continue_on_sync_failure` for intl-daily would carry the cup steps on), not settled and not pinned by a test. The chain exits 1 and pages (`OnFailure=sp-notify@%i`); its closing lines name the step and the refusal line.
