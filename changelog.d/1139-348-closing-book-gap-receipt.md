NFL (api_american_football) is taken by capture time, its 4-place agreement counted: its snapshot's de-vig is pooled, the page's averaged per book (escalated for a ruling). Its stamp is source_updated_at, the oldest over its rows, aged at exported_at, as #1077 reads it.

"Stamp after the export" is counted apart; a row with no books' session found is in "no stamp" with its cause; rows with no gap are counted by cause.
- **The window:** each family from its first stamped row, printed. Earlier rows are out and counted once; a later row with no stamp is in the "no stamp" bucket with its gap.
- **The staked rows:** PLAY in the staked line, LADDER in a line of its own: the pick's gap and its reference's bucket; "reference not the books" counted apart; desk.market_ref checked against the page's fair on the pick.
