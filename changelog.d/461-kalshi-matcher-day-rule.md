## 2026-10-12 (#461: kalshi-matcher-day-rule — ARCHITECT addendum 37 item 2, released with its day rule by addendum 44 item 1)
- src/ingestion/kalshi_match.py (new, pure): one placement for both Kalshi paths. The event's two names come from its own markets (sibling `yes_sub_title`s; the "A vs B" title is the fallback; the yes-team fallback is gone). They must fit a game one each ("St." = State), among the stored games of the series' own competition (`venue.KALSHI_SERIES_BY_COMPETITION`) on the event's day: the ticker's date read in US Eastern, a day either side. A best fit shared by two pairs is refused. Inside the one pair, a date-only ticker takes its own date before a day off; two stored rows on the date are refused under their own reason, naming the ids dedupe-matches has to merge; a date that cannot separate the pair's games refuses, printing the ticker date and our ET kickoffs. A clock ticker keeps the time gate as built and the G1/G2 suffix, and a refusal prints both times. Then the run's window decides. Two markets claiming one side are both refused. Every refusal is counted by its reason and printed.
- src/ingestion/kalshi_sync.py: `sync_kalshi_mlb` (MLB, NFL, NHL, NCAA) and `sync_kalshi_soccer` place through it. The shared path reads the series' own competition: NCAA no longer reads NFL games, nor the reverse. A series naming no competition is refused. The return keeps `matched/unmatched/ambiguous/in_play` and adds `refused` and `competition`. cli `sync-kalshi-ncaa`/`-nhl` print the refusal reason.
