- Membership (R3, R4), read on the league's 2026/27 rows in order of actual stored kickoff, then id; only kickoffs strictly after `declared_at` count. Stale orphan, cancelled, a play-off round (F3), AWD/WO (La Liga's predicate), mismatched legs, each unpriced cause, below_min_prior and no_strength are excluded by cause and take no slot. Postponed is not placed and never blocks. LIVE, SCHEDULED past its kickoff (un-ingested) and FINISHED with no 90-minute result are PENDING (a FINISHED row with no raw code is labelled apart, `no_raw_code`, open for a ruling against C1). SCHEDULED ahead is to come, printed in the pending line as R2 lists it. A regular-round game at or before the instant that is not final is PENDING too (`before_instant`): every member's walk reads it once final. A postponed game at or before the instant is counted apart. Everything else is a MEMBER: a three-way close by paper's own rule (`_guarded_three_way`) and a price from `leagues_candidate.predict_as_of` at the frozen constants. Counting stops at the 60th member. The state is accruing, waiting (a game before the 60th is not final) or ready. A stage label the code cannot place refuses the league (F3) when it kicks off at or before the 60th member, or while accruing at or before the read clock (La Liga's unplaced_through rule). The AWD / WO rows the members' walk reads as priors are counted and printed (Q2 against 158 item 2(a), open for a ruling).
