- scripts/pl_injury_exposure_receipt.py (ARCHITECT 2026-11-01 09:00 ET, addendum 196 item 2), two subcommands. Each also runs from stdin in a checkout root, the form the blocks use:
  - `exposure` (part (a), the HOST, mode=ro, no network; imports only what tag v1.6.1 holds). For every PL team of the season it reads each stored `injuries` row, prints its type verbatim, and buckets it by the fixture it names against now and the team's next PL match (scheduled or live). The buckets are past / next / upcoming before the next PL match / later / no next PL match / no fixture id / fixture not stored / held by 2+ matches / held by a match the team is not in.
    - MODEL is every stored row, its xG cut from `factors.injury_adjustment` (called, never copied). RULE is type exactly "Missing Fixture" AND next or later, the candidate's rule, counted and never applied. A "Missing Fixture" entry whose fixture cannot be placed is UNDECIDABLE, counted apart.
    - The output: a census line first, then a block per team, then totals (teams at the 25% cap under each count, the cut difference per team). Last come the PL matches on the Saturday and the four audit games with their provider fixture ids, beside the host's own closing rows and whether the stored list is the one the row consumed.
  - `lineups` (part (b), the LAPTOP). For each of the four games: the host's closing row (exports/host), the fixture id from `--fixture` or the laptop DB (mode=ro), and ONE `/fixtures/lineups` call (four at most; more refused). Answers are kept under logs/, and a kept answer makes no call.
    - Per listed name: started / bench / AMBIGUOUS / absent, absent only on a side read in full (eleven named starters, a bench, every member well-formed); else unread. Sides are told apart by the provider team id, never by name or position.
    - The entries the export does not name are counted per game. A missing count is UNKNOWN. factor_notes are printed verbatim.
