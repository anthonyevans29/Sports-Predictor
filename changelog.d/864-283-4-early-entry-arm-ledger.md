- **The join:** a fill is a closing page's game by its EVENT ticker (the market ticker less its side) against the row's `desk.order` and `kalshi_legs` tickers (match ids are machine-local); the match's closing run is the LATEST page that holds the event, ordered and timed by its file's `exported_at` (when the run's page went out), never the closing block's `start` (closing_doc writes the COVERED start, a kickoff; the run goes at that start less `RUN_LEAD_MIN`, 35 minutes); a page with no `exported_at` has no run time (labelled, law 4); kickoff is the row's `utc_date`. Pages of that export time that disagree, or an untimed page that disagrees with it, are refused, none picked. Closing files are read at the top of `--exports` and in its dated folders; `latest/` is refused, given directly or behind a dated-named folder (the 232 item 2(c) rule) (`redated.closing_rows`, the one reader).
- **One gate, early and flip alike:** the matcher booked the fill `system_matched` (a logged PLAY call on its side); a pre-closing fill with no such booking (no logged call, an off-book bet, a value shadow, 2(e)) is labelled not the arm's and counted neither early nor flip. A fill against a logged call now on the other side, on a close that PLAYs that side (a same-day side change re-logs the call in place: the Cockpit's `callKey` has no pick), is listed UNESTABLISHED, never counted (open on #862).
- **Early:** opened before the closing page went out, the page's `desk.call` PLAY, on its side (the page's `desk.order` ticker and side, else the fill's resolved role = `desk.pick`): `early` true, `early_price` the fill's entry, `closing_cost` the page's `desk.exec.cost` when the fill holds the page's own order contract (else null: the page printed no cost for that contract), `early_clv_cents = (closing_cost − early_price) × 100` (positive = early cheaper), `closing_call` the page's match id, run, pick and units. Its matcher booking (the closing call's position, 2(c)(i)) is kept.
- **Arm flip:** opened before the closing run on a match the page PASSes or PLAYs on another side, and booked `system_matched` by the matcher (a logged PLAY call on its own side): book `arm`, `call_id` none (the matcher's kept as `ledger_call_id`), so `executed_positions` over the arm's output never counts a flip; `closing_cost` null; paired with its pre-kickoff exit on the same lot (a sale is `close_fee > 0`, the fee-class rule; no sale is `close_fee` 0 on an exit of 0 or 1, or an open lot; anything else, `close_fee` missing or 0 on an exit strictly between 0 and 1, is a sale NOT READ, never "no sale"): `flip_exit_price` the exit, `flip_cost_cents = (early_price − flip_exit_price) × 100` (positive = a cost; fees excluded); otherwise both null, `flip_exit` says why, and the table's ARM FLIP NOT EXITED line counts each cause (held, exited after kickoff, sale not read, time not read; their cost is not in the flip cost, open on #862). A LADDER or VENUE close is labelled not ruled.
- **Arm breach:** a fill on an arm position's event (an early or flip fill there) opened, or sold, at or after kickoff (2(d)); a settlement is none; a lot closed after kickoff whose sale is not read is counted "not read", never "no breach"; listed by the page's match id, teams and cause only, under a CONSOLE ONLY, NOT FOR POSTING head until the architect rules where the list goes (the 235 item 2(c) precedent), never in the JSON.
- **Interest (2(f)):** an entry whose stored `type` holds the word interest is kind and book `interest`, never attributed to a call or to the arm, counted only (no amount, no rate). The Cockpit's import does not store `type` today, so today's export has none (open on #862).
- **The receipt:** `scripts/early_arm_receipt.py --ledger bd_ledger_v1_<date>.json [--exports DIR]` prints the arm's table, the unit GAMES (285 item 2(2)) with the lots a count beside them: early games, priced, the mean early CLV in cents per game (the closing page's ask less the contract-weighted early price) and the early games by the closing page's units; flip games, exited, the flip cost mean and total in cents per game (both legs' fees in); flips held to settlement (a breach, their result in units); breaches by cause; the lots above the prematch page's ask and the entries not read; interest rows; the not-the-arm's, unestablished, not-ruled, ordinary and NOT CLASSIFIED counts; the count toward thirty (games, "n of 30") and THE READ, computed at thirty games; and the breaches listed under the console-only head. Last line `EARLY-ARM-RECEIPT {json}`. Never a dollar figure, quantity, contract count, fee, stake, P&L, fill price or fill id. Writes nothing; refusals exit 2.
- **Bulletins:** `early_arm.arm_call_line(line, early)` appends the one word "early" to a call line; `early_calls` names the closing calls holding an early fill. No bulletin renderer is on main (250 step 1b unbuilt): the helper waits for it.
