- src/ingestion/kalshi_history.py:
  - **`place_chain`, `kalshi-history place-chain --anchor EVENT --ruling R (--backup PATH | --dry-run)`:** the anchored chain as ruled. The anchor is an event out of step on its own date (the identity hold's own read, `identity_holds`) ruled into place on every row on one match M, each row carrying `placed by ruling: R`. The chain is the pair's events after it in the hold's chain order, from the table only, within N1 (1-10 days after the anchor), up to the first that ENDs it (IN STEP: a row placed by the ingest; refused; mixed legs; held behind another event; held naming the anchor `unsettled`). The list is the pair's games (the games the anchor's two names fit one each, `kalshi_match.fit`, as place-held fits its match; rows that are games) from the anchor's ticker clock through its date + 11 days; fitted games of more than one pair of teams, the anchor's match with them, refuse (a second team row of one club: merge-teams first; a namesake club's game, whose names differ: rule the events by place-held), as the matcher refuses `best fit shared by two pairs of teams`; M is used by the anchor and a game a stored row outside the chain holds is used by its holder; each chain event takes the first unused game; a ruled event is counted on its own match and stops the chain where that is not the count's game. A member (every row held naming the anchor `out of step`) is placed when each row passes (a) the clock check (`MAX_TICK_DRIFT` 2h inclusive and no other unused game within 2h) or (b) the settlement bracket (settled after the game's kickoff and before the next game's in the list, or no next game in it; the game FINISHED with a stored winner; the leg's `kalshi_result` agreeing), a result mismatch refusing under both, the game SCHEDULED or FINISHED (LIVE stays held); one that passes neither stays held and the chain STOPs. The dry run prints the check per row (`PLACE <SIDE> by clock 0.0h`, `PLACE <SIDE> by settlement +3.6h, before 50551 2026-10-19 20:08 ET`). The note gains a fourth part after the ruling: `placed by the chain of <A> [k]: <how>`. Refusals exit 2 with nothing written (two rows the chain would place on one side of one game refuse too); a candle call that fails for now on any row writes nothing (all or nothing); one transaction, each row read again as a held row and its side read again; a chain with no member prints and exits 0.
  - **`list_chains`, `kalshi-history place-chain --list --series S`:** read-only, no network call, nothing written: each held row by the event its note names, that event's state and clock, and the pair's games on the held event's day (kickoff, status, gap, the rows holding each).
  - place-held's reads, candle read and write are now shared functions (`_ruled_target`, `_target_line`, `_read_cutoff`, `_placed_after`, `_write_placed`); place-held's behaviour is unchanged (its 52 console/after/refusal records over tests/test_kalshi_history_identity_hold.py are byte-identical before and after).
