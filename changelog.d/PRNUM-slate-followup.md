- deploy/hosting/closing.py (the slate stage):
  - **At most one slate RUN per tick** (`watch()` keeps `slate_tick`; `slate_watch(tick=)`, `_slate_under_lock(tick=)`). An attempt is the tick's run; a hold, a miss, a refusal, a supersession and the every-game-started miss are not. A slate due after the tick's run waits for the next tick: `record_slate_deferred` receipts it once a day as a hold (`held: one slate run per tick: the <FAMILY> slate ran this tick (the next tick runs it)`, `deferred`: that family; no step, no attempt, no page), so the LATE line's first tick is the tick that first found it due. `--dry-run` marks the second `would RUN on the next tick (one slate run per tick: <FAMILY>'s first)`. A direct `slate_watch` call (tick None) has no bound.
  - **The HELD page** (item 2): `record_slate_hold(games=, now=)` pages once per family and day, card and phone, `SLATE <FAMILY> <date> HELD for closing <FAMILY> HH:MM ET (T-35 HH:MM ET) — RUN BY HAND: block_<MMDD>_<family>_slate.txt` (`held_page_text`), when the slate is held and its family's first game not started kicks off within SLATE_GUARD_MIN (15 minutes). The hold's line carries `held_page` {text, notified}, written then even when the hold for that closing is already receipted; the tick exits 3 (NOTIFIED).
  - **The LATE line** (item 3): `LATE <m> min: due HH:MM ET, first tick HH:MM ET, run HH:MM ET`, printed whenever the run's start (`ran_at`, read in whole seconds as the receipt keeps it) is more than one cadence (60 s) after the due time, whatever held it; `<m>` from the due time to the run's start; the first tick, the earliest `queued_at` of the day's slate and slate_miss receipts. `_slate_late(..., ran_at)`; the receipt's `late_minutes` and `late` follow it.
  - **The day's counts** (item 8): `slate_calls(doc, ids)` counts the rows of the ET day's games (every call kind, by name); `beyond_day(doc, ids)` the others, `· +<k> beyond the day` after the counts when k > 0 (`beyond_text`). The page and the console line say it; the receipt keeps the day's `rows` and `calls` and `beyond_day`.
- deploy/hosting/coverage_map.py (the SLATE line):

  - **The day's file** (item 5): a file counts only when exported on the ET day.   - **The day's counts** (item 8): the competition's games of the day; `· +<k> beyond the day`; `slate.beyond` in the JSON.
