- **Ruling (verbatim):** "A Kalshi market is stored on a game only when its event fits that game: the event's two team names, taken from the event's own markets, fit the game's two teams, one each. One name alone never places a market. Names decide before time: the fit is judged among the stored games of the series' own competition on the event's day, and when the best fit is shared by games of two different pairs of teams the market is refused. The time gate and the run's window then choose only among games of that one pair, and exactly one must pass. A worse-fitting game never takes a market because the best one was outside the window or the gate. Two markets that claim the same side of the same game in one run are both refused. Every refusal is counted by its reason."
- **Day rule (verbatim):** "The event's day is the date in its ticker, read as a US Eastern day. A game is on the event's day when its stored kickoff, read in US Eastern, falls on that date or a day either side. On a series whose ticker carries no clock time that is the whole time gate: occurrence_datetime is an expected expiration, and there it places nothing and refuses nothing. Inside the one pair, a game on the ticker's own date takes the market before a game a day off; where the date cannot separate two games of the pair, the event's markets are refused. Where the ticker carries a clock time, the time gate stays as built, inside the one pair." Two stored rows of one pair on the date refuse under their own reason, naming what dedupe-matches has to merge.
