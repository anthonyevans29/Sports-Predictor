- **Ruling (verbatim, beside addendum 52 item 4, which stands):** "On a series whose ticker carries a clock time, the events of the same two names are read together. An event with no game number is out of step when no game of its two teams is on its date, or when its date is before the day of the run. Dates are read in US Eastern, and a row that is not a game is not one here. No event of the same two names dated after an event out of step is placed in that run, with or without a game number: it is refused under a reason of its own, and its line names the earliest event out of step and says why that one is. Events dated before the earliest one are placed as ruled."
- **As built (src/ingestion/kalshi_match.py):** `behind_out_of_step` reads every event of a clock series before any is placed. It groups events by their two names (case-folded) and reads every market, priced or not. "No game of its two teams on its date" is read on the pair place() would choose (the best fit on the event's day); no fit at all is no game. A market of a later-dated event of the same names that would otherwise take its place on a game is refused under "behind an event of the same two names out of step" (addendum 55 item 3, below). Its line reads `✗ <event>: refused behind <earliest>, out of step: <why>`. The event out of step keeps its own reason (no fit, no game on its date, in play, outside the window). A date-only series is outside the rule.
