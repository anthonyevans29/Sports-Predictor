- **Finding (#476), attributed to the architect's read and harness:** Kalshi's rule (`rules_secondary` of KXMLBGAME-26OCT132000CLETB-TB, read through the public API by web fetch at about 5:30pm ET): "If this game is postponed or delayed, the market will remain open and close after the rescheduled game has finished (within two days). If the game is cancelled or rescheduled to over two days away, the market will resolve to a fair price in accordance with the rules." So a postponed game keeps its event, dated the day first scheduled, and the next game of the series keeps its own. On the ALCS shapes (Game 3, 26OCT151800TBCLE, rained out and played Friday 6pm ET; Game 4, 26OCT161800TBCLE, moved to Saturday; the run Friday 11am ET), main and the switch both store the two legs of 26OCT161800TBCLE on Game 3's row. The leg guard stops it only where the later row still holds current legs of the same event. The row shows the next game's prices, and its order line carries the next game's ticker. Not established: whether it has happened on stored legs.
- **Ruling (verbatim, beside addendum 52 item 4, which stands):** "On a series whose ticker carries a clock time, the events of the same two names are read together. An event with no game number is out of step when no game of its two teams is on its date, or when its date is before the day of the run. Dates are read in US Eastern, and a row that is not a game is not one here. No event of the same two names dated after an event out of step is placed in that run, with or without a game number: it is refused under a reason of its own, and its line names the earliest event out of step and says why that one is. Events dated before the earliest one are placed as ruled."
- **As built (src/ingestion/kalshi_match.py):** `behind_out_of_step` reads every event of a clock series before any is placed. It groups events by their two names (case-folded) and reads every market, priced or not. "No game of its two teams on its date" is read on the pair place() would choose (the best fit on the event's day); no fit at all is no game. A later-dated event of the same names is refused under "behind an event of the same two names out of step". Its line reads `✗ <event>: refused behind <earliest>, out of step: <why>`. The event out of step keeps its own reason (no fit, no game on its date, in play, outside the window). A date-only series is outside the rule.
- **What it costs (ruled):** the rule only refuses. While Kalshi has not settled yesterday's market of a pair, today's is refused, and the line says so. On the evening of a rainout, the next game's event is refused though its row is right (the receipt's third shape).
