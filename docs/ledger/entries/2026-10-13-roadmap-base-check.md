- **#490, ruled in (addendum 60 item 3, verbatim, with one sentence replaced by addendum 61 item 4):** "A pull request that changes docs/roadmap.json is checked against the file on its base. For each lane both sides hold, by its lane id: an earliest_live that differs, and a due that differs on a step both sides hold, needs a moved line on the head. A step is the same step where its what is the same, word for word. The moved line's which is the word earliest_live, or the step's what, word for word; its was is the base's date and its now is the head's. A date set where the base had none needs no moved line. An earliest_live cleared needs its moved line, with now null. A lane or a step that only one side holds needs nothing from this check: the ruling the pull request quotes carries it, and I read it. The check fails the pull request and names the lane and the date. It never rewrites the file."
  - Also: "within a lane no two steps share a what."
  - Where: beside check-fragments, in the job that already reads the base.
- **#492, ruled (addendum 61 item 4, verbatim):** "Setting a first earliest_live is not a move and needs no moved line. Clearing one is a move, and it is how a failed run is written: its moved line has which earliest_live, was the date, now null, and why. In a moved line now may be null where which is earliest_live; was is always a real date. A step's due is never cleared: a step is done, moved, or removed by a quoted ruling."
- **Addendum 61 item 5:** "#494 is in: a milestone with no due_on key is refused, named." And: "One day is singular: 'in 1 day', 'OVERDUE 1 day', '(+1 more step overdue)'. Addendum 59 5(e) named the pattern, not the grammar."
