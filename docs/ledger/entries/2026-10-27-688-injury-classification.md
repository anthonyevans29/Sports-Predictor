- **Readings for the architect, as built:**
  - **Name matching is by name only.** Soccer injuries store no provider player id. Accents, case, dots, hyphens and apostrophes are normalized, and the "Initial. Surname" form is matched against a full name in either direction. Two candidates by either rule are AMBIGUOUS. A listed name that matches nothing in a side's read answers is classed "did not appear (absent from the side's answers read)" and printed UNMATCHED: the player may be out of the matchday squad, or the match may miss his name form. Every such name is on its own line for the operator to read.
  - **"Started" has a fallback.** It means in the provider's startXI. Where only /fixtures/players was read, it means minutes > 0 and substitute false; minutes > 0 with substitute null is "played (start or came on not read)". A bench name whose minutes were not read is "appearance unread", never "did not appear". A minutes value that is neither an int nor null is not the provider's shape: counted, and the name is "appearance unread".
  - **A non-recoverable side** classes only the row's own names, at most 8, with type and fixture age "(not recoverable)". The hidden count is printed; a row with no input_quality count prints it UNKNOWN, never 0. Where names were cut (or the count is unknown), listed or not is UNKNOWN: that side's not-listed players carry the warning and are counted apart (`not_listed_side_list_cut`), never in the did-not-appear total, and a worked case there prints its appearance class alone, never a "not listed" label.
  - **"Where the squad is known"** is read as the side's /fixtures/players block, read in full: minutes are in that answer only. A side read through /fixtures/lineups alone (players FAILED, empty or capped), or whose players block has a member not the provider's shape, prints "not listed, appearance unread" with its unlisted names whose minutes were not read, and is counted with the unread sides. A player outside the matchday answer and on no list cannot be seen. No season squad is read.
  - **A malformed or missing member never makes a name absent.** A side's lineups (or players) answer counts as read only when every member was the provider's shape (startXI and substitutes both lists of slots holding a player object; players entries with a player object, a statistics[0].games object and an int-or-null minutes; a player id an int or a string). Otherwise the well-formed members still classify the names they hold, but a name not found reads "appearance unread (a missing or malformed member may hide him)", never "did not appear (absent)".
  - **A provider body that will not read** (the reused lineup_history_probe.get raises on JSON that is not an object; a response that is not a list) is FAILED, counted and named. A kept payload that is not a non-empty list is asked again.
  - **The worked cases** with no closing row in the population print "no PL closing row for <home> v <away> on <date> in the population", on the console and in the JSON.
  - **SQLite sidecars (for a ruling).** The app runs the database in WAL mode. A mode=ro open of a WAL file with no live connection creates `<file>-wal` and `<file>-shm` beside it and leaves them (receipt: a scratch WAL database, a mode=ro URI connection, one `select`: `t.db-shm` and `t.db-wal` were left after close). Whether that sits inside law 5, and whether readonly_scope should change, is the architect's.
