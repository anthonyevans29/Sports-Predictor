  8. Of La Liga's predicate, only the AWD/WO half carries over (FINISHED with raw AWD/WO is excluded). Its other half (AET/PEN without a 90-minute score released) yields to R4's "no 90-minute result stays PENDING".   9. The read waits only with 60 members and a game before the 60th that is not final.
