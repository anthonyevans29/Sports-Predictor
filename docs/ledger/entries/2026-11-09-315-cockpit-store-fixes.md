- **Beyond the ruling's text, for the architect:** (1) chunk ids carry a writer token (`ledger_chunks/<rev>_<i>_<writer>`, not `<rev>_<i>`): two writers that read one header both write the same rev, and with the literal ids a failed write could leave the header naming a mix of the two (a reviewer's blocking repro); the reader takes a chunk only when its rev, index and writer match the header's. (2) The stale check compares the header's rev AND writer, and the write re-reads the header before and after its switch (up to three tries, then "NOT SAVED", and the next save merges again). (3) When the store wins on load, the local records it lacks are set aside (`bd_ledger_v1_set_aside`, counted in the note), not dropped; a merge whose save collapses two calls under the page's unchanged rule (better(): graded first, then the earliest capture) names it and sets the dropped copy aside. (4) A header that does not read whole twice is written over under a new rev, its chunks kept unread; a db read error writes nothing. (5) A localStorage journal of chunk sets lets a later save sweep chunks a closed page left. (6) scripts/cockpit_leg_audit_verify.py strips `updated_at` with the other marks.
